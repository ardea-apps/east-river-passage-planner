Notes
Rule of thumb: enter the East River 1h45m after Low Water at The Battery northbound, or 1h45m after High Water at The Battery southbound (enter at Throgs Neck). A ~7 kt boat then carries a fair current roughly 3 hours from that departure time; faster vessels get a longer window but risk outrunning the current if they depart too early.
NOAA's printed tide tables do not always correct for Daylight Saving Time — verify DST is applied when reading times, especially from data pulled outside the NOAA API's lst_ldt time zone.
